About DBM

Meet the Creative Mind Behind DBM

I am Mahnoor, an emerging graphic designer building DBM – Designs by Mahnoor as a space dedicated to clear, memorable visual communication.

My work focuses on exploring creativity through social media graphics, promotional layouts, and everyday visual storytelling. Whether a brand needs an engaging Instagram post, a menu spotlight, or a promotional flyer, I approach every canvas with curiosity, care, and respect for how color, hierarchy, and typography shape the way people feel about an idea.
